Meet The Master Stylist

Melinda's Passion For Beautiful, Healthy Crowns

"Your hair is your crowning glory. My goal is to ensure you walk out of our boutique feeling empowered, radiant, and deeply cared for."

At Melinda's Style Boutique Hair and Barber Salon, beauty is more than just styling — it is an art of transformation, self-care, and celebration of individuality. Located conveniently at Baseline Crossings in Phoenix, AZ, Melinda has built a warm, inclusive sanctuary welcoming all cultures and textures.

Whether you are preserving your natural curls with our signature moisture-locking silk press, elevating your daily look with protective knotless braids, or enjoying a precision scissor cut and barbering fade, Melinda brings over 15 years of seasoned expertise with unmatched attention to detail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Melinda's Style Boutique Hair & Barber Salon.

For silk press and natural hair care services, a deep shampoo, scalp clarifying cleanse, and steam conditioning are included in your experience! For protective braiding styles, hair should be detangled and free of heavy oils or product buildup. If you need a pre-braid wash or takedown service, please mention it when booking.
We welcome walk-ins whenever chair availability allows! However, to guarantee you have no waiting time and have Melinda's undivided attention, we strongly recommend reserving your slot online or calling us at (602) 277-4960 prior to heading over.
Yes! Melinda's Style Boutique is 100% wheelchair accessible with a zero-step entrance at Baseline Crossings LLC, wide corridors, and accessible styling stations and restrooms designed for all clients.
We provide standard hypoallergenic, pre-stretched braiding hair in popular natural colors (1, 1B, 2, 4). If you would like custom blended ombre or vivid fantasy colors, feel free to bring your favorite pack or consult with Melinda when scheduling.
